<font color=blue>**Markov Inequality: **</font>$Pr[X\geq a]\leq \frac{\mathbb E[X]}{a}$ where $X$ is non-negative random variable and $a \geq 0$

<span style="color:blue">**O**</span> notation provides an **upper bound** on a function's growth rate.  $f(n) = O(g(n))$ if $\exist c>0$ and $n_0 $such that  $0 ≤ f(n) ≤ c·g(n)$ for all $n ≥ n_0$
**<span style="color:blue">$\Omega$</span> notation** provides a lower bound on a function's growth rate. $\Rightarrow f(n) = \Omega(g(n))$ if $\exist c>0$ and $n_0 $such that $0 ≤ c·g(n) ≤ f(n)$ for all $n ≥ n₀$
**<span style="color:blue">Θ</span> notation** provides a **tight bound** on a function's growth rate. 

<font color=blue>**Master Theory:**</font>$$T(n) = aT(n/b) + f(n)$$ 
其中$a \geq 1$ 是子问题的数量, $b > 1$ 是每个子问题的规模缩减因子, $f(n)$ 是分解问题和合并结果所需的额外工作量
1.如果$f(n)=O(n^{\log_b (a)-\epsilon})$, 若存在$\epsilon >0$, 那么$T=(n^{\log_b (a)})$
2.如果$f(n)=\Theta (n^{\log_b (a)})$, 那么$T(n)=O(n^{\log_b(a)} \cdot \log(n))$
3.如果$f(n)=\Omega(n^{\log_b (a)+\epsilon})$, 若存在$\epsilon>0$, 并且 对于一个常数 $c< 1$， $af(n/b)\leq cf(n)$, 并且 $n \rightarrow \infty$, 那么便适用$T(n)=\Theta(f(n))$

<font color=blue>**Geometric sequence**</font> is an infinite sequence of the form $n,\ cn,\ c^2n,\ c^3n\ ...$ where $c \in (0,1)$ the summation is $S_t = n\frac{1-c^t}{1-c}$ This holds in general that $\sum_{t=0}^{+\infin}c^tn = \frac {n}{1-c} = O(n)$ the infinity summation is called <font color=blue>**geometric series**</font>

<font color=blue>**Divide and Conquer (Matrix Multiplication)**</font>

将 $n \times n$ 矩阵分为四个 $\frac{n}{2} \times \frac{n}{2}$ 子矩阵：$A = \begin{pmatrix} A_{11} & A_{12} \\ A_{21} & A_{22} \end{pmatrix}, \quad B = \begin{pmatrix} B_{11} & B_{12} \\ B_{21} & B_{22} \end{pmatrix}$
计算中间矩阵: $M_1 = (A_{11} + A_{22})(B_{11} + B_{22})$
$M_2 = (A_{21} + A_{22})B_{11}$
$M_3 = A_{11}(B_{12} - B_{22})$
$M_4 = A_{22}(B_{21} - B_{11})$
$M_5 = (A_{11} + A_{12})B_{22}$
$M_6 = (A_{21} - A_{11})(B_{11} + B_{12})$
$M_7 = (A_{12} - A_{22})(B_{21} + B_{22})$
结果矩阵: $C_{11} = M_1 + M_4 - M_5 + M_7$
$C_{12} = M_3 + M_5$
$C_{21} = M_2 + M_4$
$C_{22} = M_1 - M_2 + M_3 + M_6$
$C = \begin{pmatrix} C_{11} & C_{12} \\ C_{21} & C_{22} \end{pmatrix}$
时间复杂度: $T(n) = 7T(\frac{n}{2}) + O(n^2) = O(n^{\log_2 7}) \approx O(n^{2.81})$
