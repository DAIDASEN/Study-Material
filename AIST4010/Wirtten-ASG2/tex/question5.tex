\section*{Question 5: LeNet on Fashion-MNIST}

I implemented LeNet-5 using PyTorch and trained it on the Fashion-MNIST dataset. The following sections detail the results of various experiments compared to a baseline model.

\subsection*{Baseline Performance}
The baseline LeNet-5 model uses Sigmoid activation functions and Average Pooling.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Accuracy}: 87.99\%
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Experiment Results}
The following table summarizes the test accuracy for each experiment.

\begin{table}[h!]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Experiment} & \textbf{Setting} & \textbf{Test Accuracy (\%)} \\
\hline
\textbf{a. CONV Layers} & Add one CONV layer & 86.93 \\
& Remove one CONV layer & 87.24 \\
\hline
\textbf{b. FC Layers} & Add one FC layer & 87.83 \\
& Remove one FC layer & 87.95 \\
\hline
\textbf{c. Dropout} & Dropout rate = 0.25 & 88.11 \\
& Dropout rate = 0.5 & 88.05 \\
\hline
\textbf{d. Pooling Layer} & Max Pooling & 88.61 \\
& Avg Pooling, kernel size=3 & 87.33 \\
\hline
\textbf{e. Conv Window} & Kernel size = 3 & 88.40 \\
& Kernel size = 7 & 87.62 \\
\hline
\textbf{f. Output Channels} & More channels (12, 32) & 88.48 \\
& Less channels (4, 8) & 87.11 \\
\hline
\textbf{g. Activation} & ReLU & \textbf{88.89} \\
& Leaky ReLU & 88.66 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\caption{Comparison of LeNet modifications on Fashion-MNIST.}
\label{tab:results}
\end{table}

\subsection*{Best Result}
The best performance was achieved by replacing the Sigmoid activation function with ReLU.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Best Setting}: Using ReLU as the activation function.
    \item \textbf{Best Accuracy}: 88.89\%
\end{itemize}
