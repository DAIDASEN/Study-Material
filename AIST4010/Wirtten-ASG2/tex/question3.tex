If the loss plateaus or oscillates in early epochs, the usual culprits are mis-specified learning rates (too small for progress or too large causing overshoot; try an LR range test or warmup), data/label problems (missing normalization, label noise, leakage, or faulty preprocessing/augmentations), and initialization–activation mismatches (e.g., large weights with sigmoid saturating quickly). Practical issues also arise from optimization setup (wrong loss reduction/weights, detached gradients, excessive weight decay, or inadvertently training in eval mode so BatchNorm/Dropout are frozen). Finally, batching and runtime stability matter: extreme batch sizes and exploding gradients can inject NaNs; monitoring gradient norms and enabling anomaly detection help isolate the cause.
