\textbf{Definitions.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \emph{Vanishing gradients}: Backpropagated gradients decay exponentially across layers (often with saturating activations), yielding negligible updates in early layers.
    \item \emph{Exploding gradients}: Backpropagated gradients grow exponentially, causing unstable updates and divergence.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Mitigations (at least three).}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \emph{Proper initialization}: Xavier/Glorot for tanh; He/Kaiming for ReLU-family to preserve variance through layers.
    \item \emph{Activation choice}: Prefer non-saturating activations (ReLU, LeakyReLU, GELU); avoid deep stacks of sigmoid/tanh.
    \item \emph{Normalization}: BatchNorm/LayerNorm stabilize activations and gradients, enabling deeper networks and larger learning rates.
    \item \emph{Gradient clipping}: Clip global norm or per-parameter norms to cap extreme updates (mainly for explosion).
    \item \emph{Architectural aids}: Residual/skip connections and gating (e.g., LSTM) shorten gradient paths and mitigate decay.
\end{itemize}
