Severe class imbalance biases decision boundaries toward the majority and makes accuracy deceptively high while minority recall/precision collapse; calibration often degrades as well. Remedies combine data-level and loss-level strategies: resampling (minority oversampling such as SMOTE/ADASYN, or majority undersampling with stratified batching), cost-sensitive objectives (weighted cross-entropy or focal loss) that penalize minority errors more, and targeted augmentation to enlarge minority diversity. At evaluation time, per-class threshold tuning and reporting AUROC/AUPRC/F1 rather than raw accuracy give a truer picture; in practice, balanced bagging/boosting further improves robustness.
