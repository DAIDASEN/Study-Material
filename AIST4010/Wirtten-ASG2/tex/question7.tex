	extbf{Efficient Estimation of Word Representations in Vector Space (Word2Vec).} Motivation—train high-quality embeddings at web scale; Novelty—CBOW/Skip-gram with efficient approximations (hierarchical softmax, negative sampling); Intuition—distributional hypothesis: predicting context induces semantic/syntactic regularities; Implementation—\href{https://code.google.com/archive/p/word2vec/}{official archive}.

	extbf{Attention Is All You Need (Transformer).} Motivation—replace recurrence/convolution to maximize parallelism and model long-range dependencies; Novelty—pure self-attention encoder–decoder with multi-head attention and positional encoding; Intuition—each token attends globally, capturing dependencies in parallel; Implementation—\href{https://github.com/tensorflow/tensor2tensor}{Tensor2Tensor}.

	extbf{BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers.} Motivation—learn deeply bidirectional language representations for broad NLU transfer; Novelty—Masked Language Modeling and Next Sentence Prediction; Intuition—conditioning on both left and right context yields richer token embeddings transferable across tasks; Implementation—\href{https://github.com/google-research/bert}{google-research/bert}.

	extbf{MSA Transformer.} Motivation—leverage multiple sequence alignments to capture structural signals in proteins; Novelty—row/column attention across sequences and aligned positions to model co-evolution; Intuition—correlated mutations imply spatial proximity, which attention over MSAs can exploit; Implementation—\href{https://github.com/facebookresearch/esm}{facebookresearch/esm} (ESM suite).
