$$
Q^{\pi}(s_t,a_t) = r(s_t,a_t) + \gamma V^{\pi}(s_{t+1})
$$
$$
V^\pi(s_t)=\tau\log\sum_{a'}\exp\!\Bigl(Q^\pi(s_t,a')/\tau\Bigr)
$$

------

Consider the optimal policy under the maximum entropy framework:
$$
\pi(a|s)=\frac{\exp\!\bigl(Q^\pi(s,a)/\tau\bigr)}
              {\displaystyle\sum_{a'}\exp\!\bigl(Q^\pi(s,a')/\tau\bigr)}
$$
Taking the logarithm, we get:
$$
\log\pi(a|s)=\frac{Q^\pi(s,a)}{\tau}
            -\log\!\Bigl(\sum_{a'}\exp\!\bigl(Q^\pi(s,a')/\tau\bigr)\Bigr)
$$
Notice that the second term is related to the value function:
$$
V^\pi(s_t)=\tau\log\sum_{a'}\exp\!\Bigl(Q^\pi(s_t,a')/\tau\Bigr)
$$
By substituting `V` into the log-policy equation, we can express `Q` in terms of `π` and `V`:
$$
\log\pi(a|s)=\frac{Q^\pi(s,a)}{\tau}-\frac{V^\pi(s)}{\tau}
$$
$$
\Rightarrow Q^\pi(s,a)=\tau\log\pi(a|s)+V^\pi(s) \tag{1}
$$
Now, we define the intrinsic reward `r` by rearranging the Bellman equation and substituting `(1)`:
$$
r(s_t, a_t) = Q^\pi(s_t, a_t) - \gamma V^\pi(s_{t+1})
$$
$$
\Rightarrow r(s_t, a_t) = \tau\log\pi(a_t|s_t) + V^\pi(s_t) - \gamma V^\pi(s_{t+1}) \tag{2}
$$
---

### **Path Consistency Learning (PCL)**

The multi-step Path Consistency Loss is define as:
$$
\delta_{\text{PCL}}^{(k)} = \left(\sum_{i=t}^{t+k-1}\gamma^{i-t} r(s_i, a_i) \right) - \left( V^\pi(s_t)-\gamma^k V^\pi(s_{t+k}) \right)
$$
Where $\gamma$ is equal to 1.
We can substitute our derived reward from `(2)` into this definition. The term $V^\pi(s_t) - \gamma V^\pi(s_{t+1})$ forms a telescoping sum.
$$
\sum_{i=t}^{t+k-1}\gamma^{i-t}r(s_i, a_i) = \sum_{i=t}^{t+k-1}\gamma^{i-t}\tau\log\pi(a_i|s_i) + \\\sum_{i=t}^{t+k-1}\left(\gamma^{i-t}V^\pi(s_i) - \gamma^{i-t+1}V^\pi(s_{i+1})\right)
$$
The telescoping sum simplifies to:
$$
\sum_{i=t}^{t+k-1}\left(\gamma^{i-t}V^\pi(s_i) - \gamma^{i-t+1}V^\pi(s_{i+1})\right) = V^\pi(s_t) - \gamma^k V^\pi(s_{t+k})
$$
Substituting this back into the PCL error $δ$ causes the value function terms to cancel out, yielding a beautifully simple form:
$$
\delta_{\text{PCL}}^{(k)} = \left( \sum_{i=t}^{t+k-1}\gamma^{i-t}\tau\log\pi(a_i|s_i) + V^\pi(s_t) - \gamma^k V^\pi(s_{t+k}) \right) - \left( V^\pi(s_t)-\gamma^k V^\pi(s_{t+k}) \right)
$$
$$
\Rightarrow \delta_{\text{PCL}}^{(k)} = \sum_{i=t}^{t+k-1}\gamma^{i-t}\tau\log\pi(a_i|s_i)
$$
------


$$
\mathcal{L}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{(s, a) \sim \mathcal{D}} \left[ \left( \delta_{\text{PCL}}^{(k)} \right)^2 \right]
$$
