**Why Entropy?**

Entropy regularization encourages exploration and helps prevent early convergence to sub-optimal policies, as has been confirmed in practice.

loss: 
$$
loss = \max_{\pi_\theta} \mathbb{E}_{x\sim\mathcal{D}, y\sim\pi_\theta}\!\bigl[r_\theta(x,y)\bigr]
- \beta\,D_{\text{KL}}\!\bigl(\pi_\theta(y\mid\!x)\parallel\pi_{\text{ref}}(y\mid\!x)\bigr).
$$
$\mathbb{H}(x) = -\int \pi(x)\log \pi(x) d x$
$D_{\text{KL}}\!\bigl(\pi_\theta(y\mid\!x)\parallel\pi_{\text{ref}}(y\mid\!x)\bigr) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \pi_\theta(y\mid\!x)\log\frac{\pi_\theta(y\mid\!x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y\mid\!x)\bigr)}$
$D_{\text{KL}}\!\bigl(\pi_\theta(y\mid\!x)\parallel\pi_{\text{ref}}(y\mid\!x)\bigr) = -\mathbb{H}(\pi_\theta)+\mathbb{H}(\pi_\theta, \pi_{ref})$
Therefore, $ loss = \max_{\pi_\theta} \mathbb{E}_{x\sim\mathcal{D}, y\sim\pi_\theta}\!\bigl[r_\theta(x,y)\bigr]+\beta\mathbb{H}(\pi_\theta)-\beta \mathbb{H}(\pi_\theta, \pi_{ref})$
<font color=blue>$\beta\mathbb{H}(\pi_\theta)$ Shannon Entropy</font>encourages exploration,
<font color=blue>$-\beta \mathbb{H}(\pi_\theta, \pi_{ref})$ Cross-entropy </font>control don't far away from the original model.

This approach adds a cross-entropy term compared to the method in the "Bridging"

$$\max_{\pi_\theta} \sum_{x \in \mathcal{D}}\sum_{y} \pi_\theta(y|x)r_\theta(x,y) - \alpha\sum_{x \in \mathcal{D}}\sum_{y} \pi_\theta(y|x)\log\pi_\theta(y|x) - \beta\sum_{x \in \mathcal{D}}\sum_{y} \pi_\theta(y|x)\log\frac{1}{\pi_{ref}(y|x)}$$

Consider that $\sum_y \pi_\theta(y|x) = 1$

According to Lagrange multipliers

$$\mathcal{L}(\pi_\theta, \lambda) = \sum_{y} \pi_\theta(y|x)r_\theta(x,y) - \alpha\sum_{y} \pi_\theta(y|x)\log\pi_\theta(y|x) + \beta\sum_{y} \pi_\theta(y|x)\log\pi_{ref}(y|x) - \lambda\left(\sum_{y} \pi_\theta(y|x) - 1\right)$$

$$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \pi_\theta(y|x)} = r_\theta(x,y) - \alpha(1 + \log\pi_\theta(y|x)) + \beta\log\pi_{ref}(y|x) - \lambda = 0$$

Therefore: $\log\pi_\theta(y|x) = \frac{r_\theta(x,y) + \beta\log\pi_{ref}(y|x) - \lambda - \alpha}{\alpha}$

$$\pi_\theta(y|x) = \exp\left(\frac{r_\theta(x,y) + \beta\log\pi_{ref}(y|x) - \lambda - \alpha}{\alpha}\right)$$

Since that $\sum_y \pi_\theta(y|x) = 1$$\Rightarrow $$\sum_y \exp\left(\frac{r_\theta(x,y) + \beta\log\pi_{ref}(y|x) - \lambda - \alpha}{\alpha}\right) = 1$
$\Rightarrow \exp\left(\frac{\lambda + \alpha}{\alpha}\right) = \sum_y \exp\left(\frac{r_\theta(x,y) + \beta\log\pi_{ref}(y|x)}{\alpha}\right)$

$$\pi^*_\theta(y|x) = \frac{\exp\left(\frac{r_\theta(x,y)}{\alpha}\right)[\pi_{ref}(y|x)]^{\beta/\alpha}}{\sum_{y'} \exp\left(\frac{r_\theta(x,y')}{\alpha}\right)[\pi_{ref}(y'|x)]^{\beta/\alpha}}=\frac{\exp\left(\frac{r_\theta(x,y)}{\alpha}\right)[\pi_{ref}(y|x)]^{\beta/\alpha}}{Z(x)}$$
