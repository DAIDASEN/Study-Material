For Entropy-based objective function
$$\max_{\pi} \mathbb{E}_{\pi}\!\left[\sum_{t=0}^{T-1} \gamma^t \left(r(s_t,a_t) + \tau \log \pi(a_t|s_t)\right)\right]$$

Therefore we have
$$Q^{\pi}(s_t,a_t) \triangleq r(s_t,a_t) + V^{\pi}(s_{t+1})$$
$$V^{\pi}(s_t) \triangleq \mathbb{E}_{a_t \sim \pi(\cdot|s_t)}\!\left[Q^{\pi}(s_t,a_t) + \tau \log \pi(a_t|s_t)\right]$$

For Optimal case:
$$V^*(s_t)=\tau\;\log\sum_{a\in\mathcal A}\exp\!\left(\frac{Q^*(s_t,a)}{\tau}\right)$$
$$Q^*(s_t,a_t)=
\begin{cases}
r(s_t,a_t)+V^*(s_{t+1}), & \text{if }s_{t+1}\text{ is not terminal}\\
r(s_t,a_t), & \text{if }s_{t+1}\text{ is terminal}
\end{cases}$$

$$\sum_{t=0}^{T-1} r(s_t,a_t) = \sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\Bigl(Q^*(s_t,a_t)-V^*(s_{t+1})\Bigr)
\\=Q^*(s_0,a_0)+\sum_{t=1}^{T-1}\Bigl(Q^*(s_t,a_t)-V^*(s_{t})\Bigr)
\\=Q^*(s_0,a_0)+\tau \sum_{t=1}^{T-1} log(\pi^*(a_t|s_t))
\\=V^*(s_0)+\tau \sum_{t=0}^{T-1} log(\pi^*(a_t|s_t))$$

---

For non-optimal case

$$\sum_{t=0}^{T-1} r(s_t,a_t) = \sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\Bigl(Q^{\pi}(s_t,a_t)-V^{\pi}(s_{t+1})\Bigr)$$

$$=Q^{\pi}(s_0,a_0)+\sum_{t=1}^{T-1}\Bigl(Q^{\pi}(s_t,a_t)-V^{\pi}(s_{t})\Bigr)$$

$$A^{\pi}_{\text{entropy}}(s_t,a_t) = Q^{\pi}(s_t,a_t) - V^{\pi}(s_t) + \tau \log \pi(a_t|s_t)$$

$$\sum_{t=0}^{T-1} r(s_t,a_t) = V^{\pi}(s_0) + \sum_{t=0}^{T-1} \bigl( A^{\pi}_{\text{entropy}}(s_t,a_t) - \tau \log \pi(a_t|s_t) \bigr)$$

length penalty:
$\ell_t = -\lambda \bigl(1 + \alpha \cdot (t+1)\bigr)$
$Total\ reward = \sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\bigl(r(s_t,a_t)-\ell_t\bigr) \\= V^{\pi}(s_0) + \sum_{t=0}^{T-1} \bigl( A^{\pi}_{\text{entropy}}(s_t,a_t) - \tau \log \pi(a_t|s_t) -\lambda(1+\alpha\ \cdot\ (t+1)) \bigr)$
