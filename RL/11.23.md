#### Original Loss Function in Off-policy Distillation

$$L(\theta) = - \sum_{t} \left[ \frac{\pi_\theta(x_t | x_{<t})}{\pi_{\text{old}}(x_t | x_{<t})} \cdot \left( \log \pi_{\text{teacher}}(x_t | x_{<t}) - \log \pi_{\text{old}}(x_t | x_{<t}) \right) \right]$$

$$\pi^*(\tau) \propto \pi_{\text{teacher}}(\tau) \cdot \exp\left( \frac{R(\tau)}{\beta} \right)$$

$$\min_\theta D_{\text{KL}}(\pi_\theta(\tau) || \pi^*(\tau))$$

$$\begin{aligned} \mathcal{J}(\theta) &= - D_{\text{KL}}(\pi_\theta || \pi^*) \\ &= \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim \pi_\theta} \left[ \log \pi^*(\tau) - \log \pi_\theta(\tau) \right] \\ &\propto \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim \pi_\theta} \left[ \left( \log \pi_{\text{teacher}}(\tau) + \frac{R(\tau)}{\beta} \right) - \log \pi_\theta(\tau) \right] \end{aligned}$$

**最大化目标:** 

$$\mathcal{J}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim \pi_\theta} \left[ \underbrace{\frac{R(\tau)}{\beta}}_{\text{Evidence}} + \underbrace{\log \pi_{\text{teacher}}(\tau) - \log \pi_\theta(\tau)}_{\text{Prior Regularization}} \right]$$

$$A_t = A_t^{\text{Outcome}} + A_t^{\text{Distillation}}$$

$$\boxed{ A_{i,t} = \frac{1}{\beta} \cdot \underbrace{\left( \frac{R_i - \mu_{\text{G}}}{\sigma_{\text{G}}} \right)}_{\text{Normalized Outcome}} + \underbrace{\left( \log \pi_{\text{teacher}}(x_t) - \log \pi_{\text{old}}(x_t) \right)}_{\text{Dense Teacher Shaping}} }$$

$$\mathcal{L}(\theta) = - \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim \pi_{\text{old}}} \left[ \sum_{t} \frac{\pi_\theta(x_t | x_{<t})}{\pi_{\text{old}}(x_t | x_{<t})} \cdot A_{i,t} \right]$$
