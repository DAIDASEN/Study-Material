$$\mathcal{J}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim q_\theta} \left[ \sum_{t=1}^T \left( \underbrace{r(s_t, a_t)}_{\text{Outcome Reward}} + \underbrace{\log \pi_{T}(a_t|s_t) - \log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)}_{\text{Distillation Prior}} \right) \right]$$

$$\boxed{ A_{t} = \hat{R} + w \cdot \left( \log \pi_{T}(a_t) - \log \pi_{\text{old}}(a_t) \right) }$$

#### Step 1: State Sensing

1. **KL Gap **: $\text{seq\_kl} = \text{mean}(\log \pi_{\text{old}} - \log \pi_T)$
2. **Reward Fail : $\hat R$ **

#### Step 2: Gate Signal Generation

通过两个逻辑门计算干预强度：$w=w_{kl}*w_{reward}$

- $$w_{\text{kl}} = \sigma \left( \frac{\text{seq\_kl} - \text{kl\_target}}{\text{kl\_temp}} \right)$$
- $$w_{\text{reward}} = 1 - \text{clip} \left( \frac{R - \text{score\_min}}{\text{score\_max} - \text{score\_min}}, 0, 1 \right)$$

#### Step 3: Final Mapping

将双门信号融合，并映射至预设的系数区间 $[\text{min\_coef}, \text{max\_coef}]$：

$$\boxed{ w_{\text{teacher}} = w_{\text{min}} + (w_{\text{max}} - w_{\text{min}}) \cdot (w_{\text{kl}} \cdot w_{\text{reward}}) }$$
